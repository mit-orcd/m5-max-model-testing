The story of MIT's influence on AI begins almost mythically at the 1956 Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence, where John McCarthy, Marvin Minsky, Claude Shannon, and Nathaniel Rochester gathered to propose that every aspect of learning or any other feature of intelligence could be so precisely described that a machine could be made to simulate it.
