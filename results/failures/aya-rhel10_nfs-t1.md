- **NFS with TLS support**: RHEL 10 offers NFS with Transport Layer Security (TLS), enhancing security by encrypting communication between clients and servers using RPC. This feature relies on kernel TLS (kTLS), provided as a Technology Preview.
- **IPv6 address parsing**: The issue with parsing IPv6 addresses in SSHFS and NFS URLs has been fixed. Now, you can use IPv6 addresses enclosed in square brackets in BACKUP_URL and OUTPUT_URL without encountering errors.
- **NFS client-side caching**: RHEL 10 introduces FS-Cache, which improves NFS performance by caching content on the client-side. FS-Cache leverages a cache back-end (such as XFS, ext3, or ext4) to provide a persistent place for caching data.
